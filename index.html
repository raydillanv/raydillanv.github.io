About Me.

My name is Dillan Victory and I graduated from Belmont University with a major in Asian studies and a double minor in Computer Science and Japanese. I have worked with Unity and Unreal Engine, to release two games on Steam. I am always open to new opportunities, so if you have any questions or would like to get in touch, please feel free to contact me.
